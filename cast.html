Cast of Characters

The people who appear in the story, what each one did, and precisely what happened to each of them legally.

Each account uses careful words: a complaint alleged, a jury convicted, a court vacated or reversed. Being named in a report or a hearing is not the same as being charged, and being charged is not the same as being convicted.
