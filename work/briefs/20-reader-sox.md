# Brief: Reader D: Sarbanes-Oxley & the PCAOB (Phase 2)

Follow `00-common-rules.md`. Card ids `S-001`… in `work/facts/reader-sox.json`. No web. Target: 50–80 cards.

**Purpose:** fact cards for the new page "Why This Matters to You". For each provision below, card three things: (a) **what the law requires**, from the statute text; (b) **the problem Congress had in mind**, from the committee reports, CRS summary, and testimony, especially where they cite Enron; (c) **how the SEC or PCAOB first put it in place** (2002–2003), where the library covers it.

**Provisions:** §§101–109 (the PCAOB); §§201 (services outside the scope of practice), 202, 203 (partner rotation), 204, 206 (conflicts of interest / "cooling off"); §302 (CEO/CFO certification); §404 (internal control reports); §§401(a) (off-balance-sheet disclosure), 402 (loans to executives); §§802, 806, 807, 906, 1102, 1107 (document destruction, whistleblowers, criminal penalties).
