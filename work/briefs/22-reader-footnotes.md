Follow `00-common-rules.md`. Card ids `N-001`… in `work/facts/reader-footnotes.json`. No web. Target: 25–40 cards.

**Purpose:** a new pathway, "Reading the Footnotes". The owner wants to avoid fixating on Note 16. Show how several notes in Enron's 2000 Form 10-K together held pieces of the story, and what the later filings said.

**Documents and sections:**
- `enron-10k-2000` notes:
  - Note 1, Summary of Significant Accounting Policies (line ~4091): the mark-to-market / price risk management policy and the consolidation policy.
  - Note 3, Price Risk Management Activities (~4356).
  - Note 4, Merchant Activities (~4634).
  - Note 9, Unconsolidated Equity Affiliates (~5014): JEDI, Whitewing, and others; what's disclosed about them.
  - Note 14, Litigation and Contingencies, and Note 15, Commitments (~5678–5862): guarantees, and any obligations tied to stock price.

  For each note: its title, what it says in 1–3 short verbatim quotes, and **what the investigations later said about that topic** (from `powers-report-sec`, `rpt-psi-board`, `rpt-sga-watchdogs`, `batson-final`; use their disclosure sections).
- `enron-10q-q3-2001`: its related-party note and the restatement discussion.
- `enron-8k-nov-2001`: how Enron itself described what the earlier notes left out (lines 1–140, 410–800 were used before; reuse existing cards A-0xx and fn-xx where they exist, and just list them).
