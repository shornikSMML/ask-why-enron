# Glossary terms for "The Banks" page

Written by the Story Writer. Ids used on `banks.html` that are **already in** `js/glossary-data.js`: `fact-finder`, `prepay`, `funds-flow`, `three-percent-rule`. Two ids are **new**; suggested plain definitions (general knowledge, no facts about Enron):

| id | term as shown | suggested definition |
|---|---|---|
| aiding-and-abetting | aiding and abetting | Knowingly helping someone else commit a wrong, such as a fraud or a breach of duty. The bankruptcy examiner's test for a bank required actual knowledge of the wrongdoing and substantial help; "should have known" was not enough (card K-040). |
| equitable-subordination | equitable subordination / equitably subordinated | A bankruptcy court's power to move a creditor's claim behind other creditors' claims because of that creditor's unfair conduct, so it is paid later or not at all (card K-040 notes). |
