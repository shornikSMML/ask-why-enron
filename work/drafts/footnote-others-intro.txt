Enron's 2000 annual report had about twenty numbered notes, and Note 16, on related parties, is the one that drew the investigators' attention. But pieces of the same story were spread across several other notes: how Enron valued its trades and investments, which partly owned companies it kept off its books, and what it had promised to pay if its stock price fell. No single note told the whole story, so reading them together and asking how one connects to another is the skill this section practises.
