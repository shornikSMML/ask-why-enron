# Glossary terms for "Why This Matters to You"

Written by the Reform Writer for the Reference Writer. Ids used in `work/drafts/why-it-matters.html`.

**Already in `js/glossary-data.js`:** `sarbanes-oxley`, `chief-executive-officer`, `chief-financial-officer`, `internal-control`, `audit-committee`, `off-balance-sheet`, `pcaob`, `cpa`, `audit-opinion`, `auditor-independence`, `non-audit-services`, `securities-fraud`.

**New (6).** Suggested plain definitions from general knowledge. None states a fact about Enron or about how the rules work today.

| id | term as shown | suggested definition |
|---|---|---|
| material | material | Important enough that it could change the decision of a reasonable investor reading the report. A small rounding error is usually not material; hiding a large debt usually is. |
| attestation | attestation | A formal report by an outside auditor saying whether someone else's statement (here, management's assessment of its internal controls) holds up. |
| work-papers | work papers | The auditor's own files for an audit: the tests performed, the evidence gathered, and the reasons for the auditor's conclusions. |
| cooling-off-period | cooling-off period | A waiting time before someone can move from one role to a related one, such as from auditing a company to becoming one of its top officers. |
| self-regulation | self-regulation | A system in which an industry or profession writes and enforces its own rules instead of being overseen by an outside regulator. |
| peer-review | peer review | A check of one accounting firm's audit quality carried out by another accounting firm. |
