## How I checked

- **Every citation, all 240 of them** (ch1 23, ch2 18, ch3 36, ch4 28, ch5 31, ch6 47, ch7 57). A script pulled the sentence in front of each `class="cite"` link, the card it names, and the card's source and locator. Then I read each sentence against the source text at that locator. The PDFs were checked in `work/text/<id>.txt` by PDF page, and the `.txt` filings in `sources/` by line. For every claim about a named person, and every date or dollar amount, I checked the source itself, not just the card.
- **Page images (OCR sources):** JCT PDF 89, 91, 111, 112; Batson Final PDF 21, 22, 24; hearing exhibit `hrg-hec-andersen-shredding` PDF 49 (Temple's e-mail, which is image-only). All match the draft.
- **Checks on every citation:** each `data-src` is a manifest id. No `sources/candidates/` document is cited. `data-page` is right, apart from the items listed below. Only one card's source differs from its `data-src`: ch7 LeMaistre (item 7-5), which is deliberate and verified.
- **Fingerprints:** I checked the SHA-256 of all 38 library files cited in chapters 1-7 against `sources/download_log.csv`. **All 38 match.**
- **Reader tips:** Buffett does not appear in any chapter. The *Fortune* ranking appears only in the ruled wording (ch1 text and Ask Why: 2001, seventh, U.S., by revenue).
- **Outcomes:** No one is called guilty beyond what the sources show. Lay: the draft says "vacated" and "does not stand convicted" (ca5 n. 9). Andersen: "reversed" (Syllabus). Skilling: "affirmed in part, vacated in part", not reversed. Fastow: the draft says only that DOJ listed him as convicted and that the library lacks the details. The outside directors: "The library documents show no charges." Taking the Fifth (Lay, Duncan) is explicitly not treated as evidence of guilt.
