## Disagreements between sources (shown on the page)
- **Dark fiber profit:** the 10-K and Enron's Nov. 2001 8-K say $67 million; the Powers Report says "Enron recorded a $54 million gain." Neither reconciles.
- **Rhythms unwind payment:** the 10-K says Enron paid about $26.8 million. Powers says the price was set from a $30 million proposal, with a net $16.7 million paid after offsets, and that Swap Sub received "approximately $27 million cash." The SEC complaint alleges the $30 million was based on Fastow's false representation.
- **Rhythms share count:** the 10-K says 6.8 million shares; Powers says 3.4 million. Probably pre- vs post-split, but no library source states the split ratio.
- **Notional amount of Raptor derivatives:** 10-K ~$2.1 billion; Powers "over $1.5 billion" (Raptors I-III, by Nov. 2000); 8-K ~$1.9 billion.
- **Neuhausen e-mail (not used on the page):** PSI report quotes "Conflicts galore"; Batson Final Report (p. 44, checked against the image) quotes "Conflicts of interest galore." Writers quoting this e-mail should note the difference or cite one source exactly.
