# Footnotes map: Enron's 2000 Form 10-K (for the "Reading the Footnotes" pathway)

Source: `enron-10k-2000` (`sources/03-sec-filings/enron-10k-fy2000.txt`). The line numbers refer to that file. Cards are in `work/facts/reader-footnotes.json` (N-), `reader-a.json` (A-) and `work/drafts/footnote.json` (fn-).

| Note | Title | 10-K lines | What a reader could learn | What was missing (per later filings and investigations) | Cards |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Summary of Significant Accounting Policies | 4091-4266 | Consolidates "all subsidiaries controlled by Enron"; trading contracts marked to market, with unrealized gains, including those on new contracts, booked as revenue at "management's best estimate"; merchant investments at market value | How many SPEs and partnerships were kept off the books, and that three of them (Chewco, JEDI, an LJM1 subsidiary) failed the rules. The Senate PSI staff found the board accepted "the more aggressive valuation model" | N-001 to N-004, N-009, N-010; A-020, A-030, A-085 |
| 3 | Price Risk Management Activities and Financial Instruments | 4356-4633 | A trading book of about $21.5B in assets, $1,899M of income from it, $381M of securitization gains ($545M in proceeds from Whitewing), $5.5B of collateral held | How much of the fair value came from Enron's own models. The bankruptcy examiner concluded the "FAS 140" securitizations were really loans that kept the risk with Enron. Prepay loans were booked as price-risk liabilities | N-005 to N-008; A-067, A-068 |
| 4 | Merchant Activities | 4634-4687 | $601M of merchant investments at fair value (valued with appraisals and cash-flow models); gains from asset sales of $104M, $756M and $628M | The Raptors "hedged" these investments with Enron's own stock, hiding almost $1B of losses (Powers). Some asset sales went to LJM and were later bought back | N-011, N-012, N-013; A-021, A-046, A-051, A-058 |
| 9 | Unconsolidated Equity Affiliates | 5014-5151 | JEDI, JEDI II and Whitewing each 50%-owned; $197M of JEDI earnings in 2000; $632M of asset sales to Whitewing with no gain; the "Related Party" funded Whitewing and bought New Power warrants | Chewco is never named. JEDI's income included gains on Enron's own stock ($126M in Q1 2000). Whitewing's $2.4B of Osprey debt was backed by Enron stock. Senate staff called the 50% pattern a red flag | N-014 to N-019; A-031, A-032, A-035; fn-09, fn-10 |
| 10, 11 and MD&A (not in brief; added) | Preferred Stock; Common Stock; MD&A Capitalization | 5152-5215; 5326-5337; 2950-2957 | Whitewing share settlement at a $48.55 strike price; derivatives on 54.8M Enron shares (12M with JEDI, 22.5M with related parties); early-settlement triggers at a stock price of $28.20-$55.00 | No dollar amounts. The Q3 2001 10-Q showed $3.9B of possible repayments, with Osprey/Marlin triggers at $59.78 and $34.13 while the stock was at $9.00. Senate staff: "a whopping $4 billion" | N-020, N-021, N-022, N-025, N-028; A-080 |
| 14 | Litigation and Other Contingencies | 5678-5792 | Ordinary lawsuits, "not ... a material adverse impact" | No SPE or stock-price contingencies appear here | N-023 |
