## Disagreements between sources (for the Fact-Checker)
- **Skilling's promotion to President/COO:** December 1996 (SEC First Amended Complaint) vs January 1997 (indictment; SEC Second Amended Complaint; CA5).
- **Skilling's pay:** salary and bonuses 1998-2001 of "more than $14 million" (SEC) vs "more than $8 million" (DOJ press release describing the indictment).
- **Skilling's charges:** Feb 2004 indictment had 20 securities fraud counts; the July 2004 superseding indictment had 14 (CA5). Give the date with any count.
- **Bankruptcy date:** Dec 2, 2001 (most sources) vs Dec 3, 2001 (SEC Kopper complaint, para. 9).
- **Lay's stock/loan figures** cover different periods: $77.5M advances Jan-Nov 2001 (SEC); "over $77 million" Oct 2000-Oct 2001 (PSI); "over $94 million" May 1999-Oct 2001 (Batson); 918,104 shares for $26,025,000, Aug 21-Oct 26, 2001 (DOJ).
- **Director service dates** differ between PSI and Batson (Jaedicke, John H. Duncan, Blake). Batson explains that pre-1985 dates refer to HNG/InterNorth boards.
- **Kopper/Chewco loan to Southampton:** $750,000 (Kopper complaint) vs $680,000 (Fastow complaint).
- **Merrill complaint date:** file header says "February 12, 2003". The signature block, page footer and Spotlight say March 17, 2003.
- **Powers testimony:** the transcript shows no oath for Powers, but it does show one for Lay the same day.
