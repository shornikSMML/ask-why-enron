# Reader A gaps

Format: claim wanted / kind of document that would support it / importance / agent.

1. **Why Dynegy ended the merger, in Dynegy's or Enron's own filing.**
   - Claim: "On November 28, 2001 Dynegy terminated the merger, citing [reason]."
   - Document: Enron or Dynegy Form 8-K of late Nov 2001 announcing the termination, or the Dec 2, 2001 Enron press release / complaint against Dynegy. (The library has only JCT's summary, "unraveled on November 28, 2001," and Batson First Interim n. 21, "allegedly because of undisclosed liabilities.")
   - Importance: useful.
   - Agent: Reader A.

2. **The terms in the Dynegy merger 8-K exhibits.**
   - Claim: exact merger terms from the Nov 9, 2001 press release and merger agreement.
   - Document: Exhibits 99.1-99.13 to Enron's Nov 9, 2001 Form 8-K (sec.gov). The library copy of `enron-8k-dynegy-merger` is the cover filing only. Terms are available second-hand in the Q3 10-Q (used instead).
   - Importance: minor.
   - Agent: Reader A.

3. **Enron's October 16, 2001 earnings press release.**
   - Claim: exact wording and figures of the release ($618M loss; $1.01B charges; $544M component described as investment losses and termination of "structured finance arrangements"), and why its $544M after-tax figure differs from the 10-Q's "$710 million ($462 million after tax)" Raptor charge.
   - Document: Enron press release of Oct 16, 2001 (filed with the SEC as an 8-K exhibit, if one exists on sec.gov).
   - Importance: useful (the release is currently cited only second-hand, through Batson and Powers).
   - Agent: Reader A.

4. **Batson Second Interim Report (known gap; not searched for).**
   - Claim: the underlying analysis behind "six accounting techniques produced 96% of reported net income ... $10.2 billion of debt rather than $22.1 billion" (currently cited from the Final Report's summary, p. 18) and the prepay/FAS 140 detail.
   - Document: Second Interim Report of Neal Batson (Jan 21, 2003) and its Appendices E, M, Q.
   - Importance: useful (the Final Report summary is citable; the detail cannot be checked).
   - Agent: Reader A.

5. **Batson Third Interim Report (known gap; not searched for).**
   - Claim: evidence behind the examiner's conclusion that a fact-finder could find Fastow, Causey, Glisan and McMahon breached fiduciary duties.
   - Document: Third Interim Report (June 30, 2003), Appendix C (Role of Enron's Officers).
   - Importance: minor for Chapters 1-3 and 5 (the Final Report summary suffices); more important for the Cast page.
   - Agent: Reader A.

6. **Kopper and Fastow legal outcomes from primary court/DOJ documents.**
   - Claim: Kopper pleaded guilty on Aug 21, 2002 (currently only in Batson First Interim n. 8 and PSI Fishtail p. 1); Fastow's plea and sentence (no library document I read covers it).
   - Document: DOJ press releases or plea agreements for Kopper (2002) and Fastow (2004) (justice.gov). Already listed in sources/README "Still to find."
   - Importance: critical for the Cast page (not for my chapters).
   - Agent: Reader A.
