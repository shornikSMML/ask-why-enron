## `work/drafts/why-it-matters.html`: sentences that can now be made specific

1. **`#today`, second paragraph** ("This site does not yet cover what came later: how Section 404 has worked in practice and what it has cost, the PCAOB's standards and inspections, and later changes to the law..."). Most of this can now be replaced with sourced facts:
   - Sec. 404 compliance began with fiscal years ending on or after June 15, 2004 for larger companies (T-008). The SEC had estimated $1.24 billion a year for the management report alone (T-012). In 2006 GAO found costs fell disproportionately on smaller companies (T-046, T-047). The SEC's 2007 guidance moved management to a top-down, risk-based evaluation (T-016 to T-019).
   - In 2010 the Dodd-Frank Act exempted companies that are neither large accelerated nor accelerated filers from the auditor attestation (T-026; use the exact wording). A 2012 amendment added emerging growth companies (T-027).
   - In 2010 the Supreme Court held that the Board members' double protection from removal was unconstitutional but kept the Board, whose members the SEC can now remove at will (T-041 to T-044).
   - In 2008 GAO found the Big Four still audited almost all large companies (T-052, T-053).
   - Keep one caveat: PCAOB inspections of the Big Four and later PCAOB standards other than AS 2201 are still not covered (gap 4). The phrase "a picture of what Congress decided in 2002, not a guide to today's rules" should be softened, not deleted.
2. **`#today`, first paragraph** ("This site's source library covers the law and its first year, 2002 and 2003"). This is now out of date; the library now reaches 2010 (*Free Enterprise Fund*) and includes current U.S. Code text (2024 edition) and the current AS 2201. It could add GAO 2008 (T-052) after the 2003 concentration sentence.
3. **`#s302` "What it means for you"** ("two named executives must sign. They may ask how you got those numbers"). This can be made specific: under the SEC rule the officers also certify that they evaluated the company's *disclosure controls* within 90 days and told the auditors and audit committee about control weaknesses and any fraud involving management (T-004, T-005). This is why people who prepare numbers are asked about controls. The SEC made 906 certifications public exhibits (T-015).
4. **`#s404` "What it means for you"** ("Section 404 made it part of what management reports to investors and the auditor checks"). Add that one material weakness means management may not call controls effective (T-009); the auditor gives its own opinion on effectiveness (T-023). Add that since 2010 the auditor attestation does not apply at smaller companies (T-026), but management's report still does. A reconciliation that fails can therefore be a disclosed "material weakness."
5. **`#pcaob` "What it means for you"** ("under the 2002 law, your firm's work could be inspected by the Board..."). The hedge "under the 2002 law" can go. *Free Enterprise Fund* (2010) confirms the Board continues to function (T-042), and its Syllabus records an actual inspection and critical report (T-039). Inspection frequencies today are still unsourced.
6. **`#independence` "What it means for you"** (audit-team member becomes CFO, etc.). Add the SEC's rotation periods: lead and concurring partners rotate after 5 years with a 5-year time out; other partners after 7 years with a 2-year time out (T-030, T-031). Add the three principles, stated by the SEC in its rule as well as by the Senate (T-033). Tax work is allowed only with audit-committee pre-approval (T-035). Audit partners can't be paid for selling non-audit services (T-036).
7. **`#whistleblowers` "What it means for you"** ("Section 806 was written to protect employees who report it through the channels it names"). Add that since 2010 a separate law lets the SEC pay a whistleblower 10 to 30 percent of sanctions collected in cases over $1 million, and protects against retaliation with double back pay (T-054, T-055). Note that auditors face limits on claiming awards (T-056).
8. **Ask Why aside.** No change needed. Optionally connect it to T-021 (controls can be defeated by collusion or management override).
