# Phase 2 plan (proposal for the owner's approval)

## What Phase 2 adds
1. **Lenses:** four switches on every chapter page: *Follow the Money*, *The Auditors*, *The Board*, and *Who Knew What, When*.
2. **Pathways:** five guided routes through the whole site.
3. **"Why This Matters to You":** what Sarbanes-Oxley changed, and what it means for anyone entering business or accounting.

The Phase 1 rules still apply: every fact comes from the library, every claim about a person is cited, the Fact-Checker reviews everything, and everything is logged.

---

## 1. Lenses (how they work for a reader)
- **A lens bar** sits at the top of each chapter, with four buttons. A reader can turn on one lens at a time, or none.
- **When a lens is on:**
  - paragraphs relevant to that lens are highlighted, using the lens tags the Story Writer already added (money 43, auditors 33, board 16, who-knew 26);
  - short **lens notes** appear in the margin. On a phone they open with a tap. Each note is 1–3 sentences reading that paragraph through the lens, for example: "Follow the money: this $30 million came back to LJM2 within six months …";
  - a **lens summary** box appears at the end of the chapter: three or four bullet points plus one lens-specific "Ask Why" question.
- **"Who Knew What, When"** also shows a small dated strip at the top of the chapter: who is documented as knowing what, and on what date, from the Timeline and the fact cards.
- **Lens notes follow the story's rules:** facts only from checked fact cards, the right verbs, citations. A lens adds interpretation of *emphasis*, not new accusations.

## 2. Pathways (proposed five)
| Pathway | For whom | Route (examples) |
|---|---|---|
| **A First Reading** | Anyone, about 45 minutes | Ch 1 → 2 → 3 (short version) → 5 → 7, with 3 Cast stops |
| **Mark-to-Market Explained** | Accounting beginners | Glossary: mark-to-market → Ch 2 diagram → the Raptor diagram → the restatement chart → Ask Why |
| **The Special Purpose Entities** | Readers who want the mechanics | Ch 3 → the Chewco/LJM diagram → The Footnote (key annotations) → Cast: Fastow, Kopper |
| **Andersen's Fall and the Big Five to Big Four** | Future auditors | Ch 6 → Cast: Duncan, Temple, Berardino → Timeline (auditor tag) → the Supreme Court opinion |
| **From Enron to Sarbanes-Oxley and the PCAOB** | Business students | Ch 7 → Why This Matters → the Timeline's reform events |

- **Each pathway** is a sequence of "stops" across existing pages. It shows a small "Pathway: stop 3 of 8 → next" bar, a one- or two-sentence bridge at each stop, and a closing question.
- **Bridges add no new facts.** They only connect stops, so the Fact-Checker checks them for accuracy and tone.
- **Instructor note:** each pathway has an estimated reading time and a printable one-page handout.

## 3. "Why This Matters to You" (new page)
**Sections:**
- CEO/CFO certification (SOX §§302, 906)
- Internal control reporting (§404)
- The PCAOB (§§101–109)
- Auditor independence (§§201–203, 206)
- Document destruction and whistleblowers (§§802, 806, 1107)

**For each provision:**
- **What the law requires**, cited to the statute itself.
- **The Enron problem Congress had in mind**, cited to the Senate and House reports and the hearings. This links back to the chapters.
- **What it means for you**, in plain language: a student who becomes an accountant, auditor, analyst, manager or employee. This part may explain concepts from general knowledge, but it makes no specific claims about how rules work today unless the library supports them.

**Honest limit:** the library covers the law and its first year (through 2003). Later developments, such as how §404 has been applied since and how PCAOB inspections have worked in practice, are not in the library. The page will say so. The agents will log them in `gaps.md` as possible future sources rather than state them from memory.

## Order of work
- **Wave A (in parallel):** Reader D, the Lens Writer, the Pathways Designer, the Site Builder (UI with sample data), and the Image Researcher (non-SOX items).
- **Checkpoint:** the Fact-Checker checks the S-cards; at the same time, a second Fact-Checker checks the lens notes.
- **Wave B:** the Reform Writer and the reform diagrams, while the Lens Writer and the Pathways Designer make their fixes.
- **Wave C:** the Fact-Checker reviews the reform page and pathways; the writers fix; the Fact-Checker re-checks until everything is PASS.
- **Wave D:** the Site Builder integrates and tests (laptop, projector, phone; light and dark; keyboard); the Scribe updates the log; I do a final check and check in with you.

**Estimate:** about 2–3 hours of agent time.
